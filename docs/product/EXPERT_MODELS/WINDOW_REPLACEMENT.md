Lifecycle: `EXPERT_MODEL`

## JTBD
Replace old windows; install windows into new openings; improve thermal/acoustic comfort; determine whether full replacement is actually necessary when the complaint may be caused by adjustment, seals or mounting joints.

## QUOTE_REQUIRED
Ask only what materially changes quote/scope: goal; object type; number of openings; approximate dimensions or unknown; photos; geography. For noise/heat complaints, do not force the user to choose technical components — contractor must propose them.

## INSTALLATION_REQUIRED / site inspection
Exact opening dimensions and geometry; quarters/reveals; substrate condition; mounting gaps; junction condition; ability to install sill/flashing/reveals; hidden damage after dismantling; access/delivery/lifting constraints.

## Contractor must propose / confirm
- profile system and series, not brand only;
- glass unit specification / chamber count and relevant options;
- hardware and opening scheme;
- mounting-joint / installation method;
- dismantling and disposal;
- sill, interior reveals and exterior flashing;
- delivery / lifting;
- opening preparation if required;
- measurement conditions;
- warranty and what it covers;
- contract terms and final-price fixation;
- alternatives when the user's goal can be achieved without full replacement.

## Technical model
A window offer is a system, not just a profile. For comparison, use the combination of frame/profile, glass unit, hardware/opening scheme and installation/junction solution. Do not infer acoustic or thermal performance from profile chamber count alone. If the complaint is draft/cold, exclude repairable causes (adjustment, seals, local mounting-joint defect) before asserting that replacement is required.

Normative references verified for the model: ГОСТ 23166-2024 (window and balcony blocks) and ГОСТ 30971-2012 for mounting joints/adjacency. Manufacturer/market guidance also consistently treats dimensions, configuration, profile, glass unit and installation as price/specification drivers.

## Price factors
Opening size and quantity; opening scheme and number of operable sashes; profile system; glass-unit configuration and special glass; hardware; non-standard shape/colour; dismantling complexity; opening condition; reveals/sill/flashing dimensions; floor/access/lifting; delivery distance; waste removal; additional restoration.

## Hidden extras commonly found in market offers
Reveals, sill, flashing, disposal, lifting, delivery, difficult dismantling, opening preparation, expanders/connectors, mosquito screens, child locks and non-standard glass/hardware. A label `под ключ` is not sufficient: compare the explicit scope.

## Technical red flags
- acoustic/thermal promise based only on profile chamber count;
- no glass-unit specification;
- no hardware/opening scheme;
- no mounting/junction description for an installation quote;
- replacement recommended for a draft complaint without diagnosis;
- quote ignores the opening condition and post-dismantling risk;
- final performance guaranteed before exact measurement/site inspection.

## Commercial red flags
- price `от` presented as final;
- construction-only price compared with a full installed package;
- `под ключ` without itemized included/excluded works;
- final price can change without defined triggers;
- no warranty scope or contract fixation.

## Non-comparable offers
Different glass units, hardware/opening schemes or quantities; installation included vs excluded; reveals/sill/flashing included vs excluded; dismantling/disposal included vs excluded; construction-only offer vs installed package; fixed final price vs `от` price.

## What the service must not claim without confirmation
Exact acoustic/thermal result; necessity of full replacement; exact final dimensions; opening structural condition; absence of hidden restoration; compliance/quality of a contractor's mounting work; final price before measurement when site-dependent items remain unknown.

## Contractor Brief requirements
Request one fixed post-measurement total plus explicit specification: profile system, glass unit, hardware/opening scheme, installation, dismantling, reveals, sill, flashing, delivery/lifting, disposal, exclusions, extra-cost triggers, schedule, measurement, warranty and contract terms.

## Search qualification
Target launch geography: Moscow + Moscow Oblast. Shortlist 3, max 5. Required signals: window installation in target geography; measurement; installation rather than product-only sales; ability to disclose profile/glass/hardware and work scope. Exclude product-only sellers, irrelevant repair-only contractors for replacement jobs, out-of-geo contractors and listings that cannot provide an itemized installed quote.

## Research evidence summary
Current Rosstandart entries confirm ГОСТ 23166-2024 and ГОСТ 30971-2012. Moscow/MO offers reviewed show materially different bundling: some include dismantling, mounting, reveals, sill, flashing and disposal in `под ключ`, while others list sill/reveals/flashing/disposal as separate options. This confirms that normalization must compare explicit scope, not headline price.
